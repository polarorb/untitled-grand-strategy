# Economic Systems Competition

Status: sketch
Pillar: 4

## Core idea

Market and planned economies are **different rule sets**, not a slider.

- **Market economies**: production responds to budgets and demand with lag;
  the government steers via spending, taxes, and procurement. Strong at
  consumer goods, innovation, and long-run growth; guns-vs-butter tradeoffs
  show up as inflation and electoral pain rather than shortages.
- **Planned economies**: the player sets output targets directly (five-year
  plan cadence — a natural monthly/annual mechanic). Strong at directed
  heavy industry and crash programs; weak signals produce misallocation,
  shortage chains, and plan-fulfillment lying that corrupts your *own*
  statistics (your intel screen about yourself has error bars).

Shared vocabulary underneath so systems interoperate: industrial capacity,
resources (oil, steel, rare inputs), labor, and **budget pressure** — the
arms race is a spending contest both sides can lose at home.

## Blocs & trade

Marshall Plan / COMECON-style bloc economics: aid packages buy alignment
(influence doc), trade dependence creates leverage, embargoes are escalation
moves. Trade routes matter for interdiction later.

## Research & the races

Tech tree with era gates; crash programs (Bomb, thermonuclear, missiles,
space) as focused big-spend projects with spy-theft shortcuts (espionage
doc). The Space Race is a prestige pump on this system.
